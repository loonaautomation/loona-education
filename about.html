About Loona Education

Loona Education · Loona Automation

An AI Teachers Institute, not another video course

Loona Education is an online training platform that teaches school teachers to actually use AI in their classrooms — not by watching lectures, but by doing real work every day. Most teachers do not need more theory about artificial intelligence. They need someone to show them, step by step, how to plan a lesson with AI, design a classroom post, create a story or a song for their students, and present their work with confidence. That guided, practical training is exactly what Loona Education provides, through its AI Teachers Institute programme.

The programme: 452 tasks, two levels

The programme contains 452 practical tasks across two levels. Level 1, Foundation, has 251 tasks over 41 working days and starts from zero: designing posts with ChatGPT, creating AI songs and stories, holding voice sessions with AI partners, growing a professional network, recording first interviews and completing ten guided AI coding projects from Day 15 onward. Level 2, Advanced, has 201 tasks over 41 working days and goes deeper, with longer voice sessions, professional interviews, testimonial videos and 25 AI coding projects — unlocking only when Level 1 is complete. In total there are 13 practical task types, from ChatGPT design posts and AI songs to Canva designs, TEDx talk reviews, voice sessions and coding projects.

How learning actually happens here

Live online classes run Monday to Friday in the evening, after school hours, because the students are working teachers. Each day follows the same rhythm: attend the live class, pick the day's task in your student dashboard, follow the help guide that shows exactly what to do and how it will be checked, publish your work publicly, and submit your links. Facilitators and mentors review every task one by one — real people, not an algorithm — so no student's work waits long for feedback. Complete 100 percent of a level and your certificate for that level unlocks; the certificate is earned by finished, reviewed work, not by attendance alone.

Trial, honesty and who this is for

You can begin with a 7-day free trial, with no credit card required, and see the dashboard, the tasks and the class rhythm before deciding anything. Loona Education is built for school teachers at every starting point — including teachers who have never used an AI tool before and teachers who feel technology is moving faster than their training. If you can attend an evening class and complete one guided task a day, this programme was designed for you.

Loona Education is part of Loona Automation, founded by Aurang Zeb Loona, himself a school teacher and AI teacher trainer from Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan. He teaches in a real classroom every day, trains teachers in AI, and built this institute on the method he uses with his own students: explain clearly, practise immediately, review kindly, and let explaining a thing out loud make it stick.
